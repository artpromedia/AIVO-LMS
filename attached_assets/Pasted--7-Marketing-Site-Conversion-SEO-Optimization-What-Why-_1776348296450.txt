#7 - Marketing Site Conversion & SEO Optimization
What & Why
The marketing site at apps/marketing needs to drive conversions for aivolearning.com. Currently CTAs link to redirect pages, there's no analytics tracking, SEO metadata is minimal, and there's no sitemap or robots.txt.

Done looks like
Every CTA button links to a real destination: B2C CTAs to signup with plan pre-selected, B2B CTAs to contact/demo form
Pricing section plan buttons link to registration with plan parameter
Contact page form submits to a real backend endpoint (not just mailto links)
Demo request form captures company, role, email, school/district size
Newsletter/waitlist signup connected to backend storage
GA4 analytics integrated with conversion events on CTA clicks, form submissions, pricing selection, signup initiation
Every page has proper title, meta description, Open Graph tags, Twitter Card tags, canonical URLs
Organization JSON-LD structured data on landing page
sitemap.xml generated via Next.js sitemap.ts
robots.txt present
All 10+ pages have unique, keyword-rich meta descriptions
No placeholder content (Lorem ipsum, TODO, TBD) remains
Mobile responsive: hamburger menu, stacking grids, 44px touch targets
Images use Next.js Image component with proper dimensions and priority
Fonts loaded via next/font
Out of scope
Facebook/Meta Pixel (can be added later)
Full blog CMS integration
A/B testing infrastructure
Tasks
Wire all CTA buttons — Connect Hero, StickyHeader, CTASection, pricing plan buttons to real destinations. B2C CTAs go to signup on apps/web with plan pre-selected. B2B/district CTAs go to contact/demo form.

Build contact & demo forms — Replace mailto links on the contact page with a real form that submits to a backend endpoint. Create POST endpoint in comms-svc or admin-svc for contact submissions and demo requests. Store submissions in DB.

Newsletter/waitlist capture — Add email capture in footer or CTA section, connected to a backend endpoint that stores email subscriptions.

GA4 analytics integration — Add Google Analytics 4 via next/script in root layout. Add conversion tracking events on CTA clicks, form submissions, pricing plan selection, and signup initiation.

SEO metadata — Add unique title, meta description, OG tags, Twitter Card tags, and canonical URLs to every page via Next.js metadata exports. Add Organization JSON-LD to the landing page.

Sitemap & robots.txt — Create sitemap.ts and robots.txt in apps/marketing/src/app/.

Placeholder content cleanup — Search all marketing pages for Lorem ipsum, TODO, TBD, empty paragraphs, and test data. Replace with real copy.

Mobile responsiveness audit — Verify responsive breakpoints, hamburger menu, stacking grids on mobile, and 44px touch targets on all CTAs.

Performance optimization — Ensure all images use Next.js Image with proper dimensions/priority. Verify fonts loaded via next/font for optimal LCP. Add lazy loading for below-fold sections.
