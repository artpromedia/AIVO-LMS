RBAC and tenant scope

Every request must be scoped to one school tenant only. The school admin must never see other schools, district-wide data, or cross-tenant learners unless their role explicitly grants that scope.

Required guard:

requirePageRole(...)
schoolTenantId required
parent district context optional but non-leaking

RBAC

This is the biggest risk area.

Teacher access must be:

teacher belongs to class
learner belongs to that class
teacher can access only learners assigned to their roster/classroom

Do not rely only on same tenant.

Required guard:

requireTeacherClassroomScope(teacherId, classId)
requireTeacherLearnerScope(teacherId, learnerId)

This closes the IDOR risk.
