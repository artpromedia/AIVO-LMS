# AIVO — Learn, Teach, Support

> One app. Five roles. The whole household, the whole classroom, the whole care team.

AIVO is one learning app for everyone who supports a learner. Sign in once and switch between any of the roles you hold:

- **Learner** — daily lessons, AI tutor, homework helper, missions, and progress that grows with you.
- **Parent** — see what your children are working on, approve content, message teachers, and track real progress.
- **Teacher** — plan lessons, see class insights, monitor baselines, and message families without leaving the app.
- **Therapist** — caseload, IEP goals, session notes, and progress reports for the learners you support.
- **Caregiver** — observe day-to-day activities, see goals from the care team, and stay in the loop.

School and district administrators also have a dedicated surface for roster, billing, and compliance work.

## Why one app

We refuse to make families install three different apps just because Mom is a parent at home and a teacher at work. AIVO is a single shell that re-skins itself for whichever role you're acting as, with a calm, sensory-friendly design across every surface.

- Switch roles in two taps — your active role tells the app which slice to show.
- Cross-cutting notifications, messages, settings, and billing share one home no matter which role you're in.
- Built for accessibility from day one: WCAG-2.1-AA contrast, large-text mode, reduced-motion mode, and high-contrast palettes.

## Privacy

AIVO is built for K-12 and family use. We meet COPPA, FERPA, GDPR-K, and SOC 2 Type II expectations, and we never sell student data.

## What's included

- AI tutor and homework helper with calm, age-appropriate guardrails.
- IEP-aware progress for parents, teachers, therapists, and caregivers.
- Baseline diagnostics that adapt over time.
- Family messaging and consent tools — without a separate "parent" app to install.
- Therapist caseload and session-notes workflow.
- Caregiver observations and goal tracking.

## Built for the whole team

AIVO is the same app whether you're a 7-year-old running their daily mission, a parent reviewing the week, a teacher planning tomorrow's lesson, a therapist updating an IEP goal, or a caregiver dropping a quick observation. One sign-in, one app, the right slice for whoever you are at the moment.
