# What's new in this release

- **One app, five roles.** Learners, parents, teachers, therapists, and caregivers all sign in to the same AIVO app and switch roles in two taps — no more installing a separate "parent" or "teacher" app.
- **Universal links.** Tapping an `aivo.app` link from a notification or a shared message takes you straight to the right slice of the right learner, switching your active role automatically if needed.
- **Calmer notifications.** Every push now tells the app which role you need to be in to act on it, so you land where the work is — never on a "wrong role" screen.
- **Sensory-friendly polish.** Improved high-contrast palette and a tighter reduced-motion mode for learners who need them.
