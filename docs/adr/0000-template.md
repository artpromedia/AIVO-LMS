# NNNN — Short, descriptive title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** @handle1, @handle2
- **Related:** issue/PR/ADR links (optional)

## Context

What is the problem we're trying to solve? What are the relevant
constraints (regulatory, performance, organisational, technical debt)?
Describe the situation in enough detail that a reader unfamiliar with
the system can follow the rest of the document.

Avoid prescribing a solution in this section.

## Decision

State the decision in one or two sentences, in the active voice
("We will …"). Then expand on the specifics: which components are
affected, how the new behaviour works, what defaults change.

If the decision is conditional ("we will use X for service A but not
service B"), say so explicitly.

## Consequences

Document the trade-offs honestly.

- **Positive:** what gets better.
- **Negative:** what gets worse, what risk we accept, what we now have
  to maintain.
- **Neutral / follow-ups:** things this ADR does not yet address but
  someone should later.

## Alternatives Considered

List the realistic alternatives that were on the table and why they
were rejected. A one-liner per option is fine; the goal is to show the
decision was deliberate, not arbitrary.

- **Option A — …** Rejected because …
- **Option B — …** Rejected because …
