# Design principles

1. **Playful, not chaotic** — colorful accents over calm surfaces.
2. **One clear action** — one primary CTA per learner step.
3. **Big touch comfort** — minimum 56px touch targets in Sprout/Spark.
4. **Audio-first support** — read-aloud controls are always available.
5. **Progressive disclosure** — reveal complexity in small chunks.
6. **Emotionally supportive feedback** — mascot coaching and friendly recovery copy.
7. **Accessible by default** — high contrast, dyslexia-friendly font option, reduced-motion behavior.

## Do / Don't

- Do: pair color with icon/text status.
- Don't: rely on color alone.
- Do: keep rounded geometry (12px+).
- Don't: introduce sharp-edged, dense card systems in learner flows.
